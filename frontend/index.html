  • Never pay upfront fees to personal accounts: Genuine agencies only collect approved fees with official printed receipts after visa issuance.
  • Beware of "100% Guaranteed Visas": No third party or agent can guarantee foreign government visa approvals without official interviews.
  • Always call 1989: Before handing over money or your passport, call the SLBFE 24/7 hotline 1989 to verify the agent's licence.
