# Impact Statement: JobShield LK

> **Protecting Sri Lankan migrant workers and their families from predatory foreign-employment recruitment fraud through real-time, drift-aware scam intelligence.**

---

## 1. Who is Protected

JobShield LK safeguards vulnerable, aspirational Sri Lankan migrant job seekers and the extended families whose livelihoods depend on them:

- **Rural and First-Time Job Seekers:** Over 70% of outbound migrant workers come from rural and semi-urban communities (e.g., Kurunegala, Kandy, Galle, Batticaloa, Anuradhapura). Navigating recruitment primarily via unmonitored channels—Facebook public groups, TikTok recruitment influencers, and viral WhatsApp forwards—they lack ready access or digital fluency to cross-reference SLBFE licensing registries.
- **Youth & Skilled/Semi-Skilled Applicants:** Young workers pursuing newly opened, high-wage corridors (South Korea, Japan, Israel, Romania, Poland, Canada) who are aggressively targeted with fraudulent work-visa offers demanding large upfront "agency deposits".
- **Female Domestic Workers:** Women heading to the Middle East who frequently face predatory intermediaries charging illegal processing fees despite statutory employer-paid migration mandates.
- **Interdependent Extended Families:** Spouses, aging parents, and children who finance departure fees by liquidating family land, pawning gold jewellery, or taking high-interest loans from informal money lenders (10–25% monthly interest).

---

## 2. How Many People Could Be Reached

- **Addressable Population:** In 2024, a record **~313,000 Sri Lankans officially departed for foreign employment** (SLBFE). Because multiple job seekers apply for every published opening, the active pool exposed to overseas job postings is estimated at **1.2 to 1.5 million people annually**.
- **Digital Penetration:** Over 75% of prospective migrant workers regularly access Meta platforms (WhatsApp, Facebook) and TikTok via mobile devices.
- **Target Adoption Funnel:**
  - **Year 1 Target (Pilot):** **100,000+ citizens reached**, with **50,000+ job advertisements audited** via the self-service web checker, WhatsApp message-forwarding verification, and assisted screening across 25 SLBFE District Extension Offices.
  - **Year 2 Scale:** **400,000+ checks annually**, embedding verification into mandatory pre-departure orientation, district Divisional Secretariats, and migrant trade union networks.

---

## 3. Measurable Harm Prevented

Recruitment fraud causes immediate financial insolvency, debt bondage, and human trafficking. JobShield LK interrupts this pipeline before money changes hands:

- **Direct Financial Loss Interception:**
  - Individual victims are extorted for **LKR 150,000 to LKR 1,500,000** ($500–$5,000 USD) in fake "registration", "visa processing", and "guaranteed placement" fees.
  - Documented benchmarks: In early 2025, 10 victims lost **LKR 14.9 million** to a single fake Canada scheme; an unlicensed syndicate defrauded **LKR 200 million** for bogus European hospitality jobs. Between Oct 2025 and Feb 2026, the SLBFE recovered and refunded **LKR 194.5 million**—a small fraction of total illicit extraction.
  - **Projected Financial Savings:** Intercepting 1,500 fraudulent transactions in Year 1 preserves **over LKR 500 Million (~$1.7M USD)** directly for working-class families.
- **Systemic & Human Harms Mitigated:**
  - **Debt Bondage & Asset Stripping:** Prevents distress asset liquidations (pawned gold, mortgaged ancestral land) and predatory microfinance debt cycles when promised jobs fail to materialize.
  - **Human Trafficking & Cyber Slavery:** Prevents victims from traveling on tourist/visit visas into unregulated foreign jurisdictions or forced-labor cyber-scam sweatshops (e.g., Southeast Asian cyber-trafficking corridors).
- **Core Pilot Metrics:**
  1. *Direct Capital Saved:* $\sum (\text{Confirmed High-Risk Scams} \times \text{Quoted Advance Fee})$.
  2. *Interception Rate:* Ratio of verified fake ads flagged before payment deadlines.
  3. *Triage Acceleration:* Reduction in SLBFE Special Investigation Unit (SIU) case intake and verification time from days to seconds.
  4. *Tactic Adaptation Latency:* Time (hours) between scam tactic drift detection and retrained model deployment.

## 4. Realistic Path to Adoption

JobShield LK is engineered for zero-friction, zero-marginal-cost public sector adoption:

| Horizon | Milestone & Execution Channels |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Month 0–1:**<br>*(Public Web Checker)* | Deploy the free web checker in Sinhala, Tamil, and English. Every scan output delivers transparent warning flags and direct one-click verification against the official SLBFE licensed agency register (`slbfe.lk`) and the **1989** National Migrant Hotline. |
| **Month 2–3:**<br>*(SLBFE SIU Pilot)* | Integrate the review queue into the SLBFE Special Investigation Unit (SIU) dashboard. Officers triage citizen reports with pre-computed risk scores, transforming daily investigative verdicts into active retraining data. |
| **Month 4–6:**<br>*(Grassroots & WhatsApp)* | Launch a lightweight WhatsApp verification bot for one-touch message forwarding. Equip SLBFE Development Officers across 25 District Offices to audit flyers during pre-departure registration. |
| **Month 7–12:**<br>*(Ecosystem Partnerships)* | Partner with the Central Bank of Sri Lanka (CBSL) "Be Scam Proof" campaign and Sri Lanka CERT\|CC. Share aggregated scam patterns and suspicious bank/wallet accounts with the Police Cyber Crimes Division and social media platforms for proactive takedowns. |

**Operational Sustainability:** The entire inference and drift-monitoring pipeline runs on a single low-spec CPU instance (< $15/month), ensuring long-term institutional viability for government and non-profit operators without recurring proprietary LLM API costs.
